# AIPolicyAssessment2

Economics of Artificial Intelligence Assessment 2 - AI Policy Essay
This repository holds my submission for Econ. of AI Assessment 2. It includes the different versions of essays adapted through the process of producing this policy proposal. Each version reflects a different perspective, with earlier versions focusing on broader concepts before converging on a formal policy brief.

- **Version 1** — A brief outline of the key topics to be addressed in the proposal.
- **Version 2** — A narrative essay looking back on the policy proposal from a position in the future (2035).
- **Version 3** — A combination of narrative and analytical writing, with a larger focus on AI policy from a government lens and its effects on economic concepts.
- **Version 4** — The final submission: a formal government policy brief analysing AI's labour market impacts across three economic channels, evaluating three policy options, and recommending a phased implementation package with a risk register and measurable KPIs.
